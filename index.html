  • Use high-resolution images (at least 300 DPI for scanned documents)
  • Dark text on light background gives the most accurate results
  • Avoid blurry, skewed or heavily compressed images
  • Printed text is recognised more accurately than handwriting
  • For handwriting, use English mode and ensure clear, large lettering
