How to use this OCR tool

  1. Select the language of text in your image
  2. Upload your image (drag & drop or click to browse)
  3. Wait a few seconds while text is recognised locally in your browser
  4. Copy the extracted text or download it as a .txt file

Tips for best results

  • Use clear, high-contrast images with dark text on light background
  • Avoid blurry, skewed or heavily compressed images
  • Printed text gives more accurate results than handwriting
  • Higher resolution images produce better output
